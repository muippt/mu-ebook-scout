THIRD-PARTY SOURCE LICENSES
===========================

mu-ebook-scout aggregates *links* from the book sources listed below.
The project itself distributes no book content; it only indexes and
surfaces links produced by each source's public interface. All
copyrights for the indexed material belong to their respective owners.

Source-by-source license notes
------------------------------

Project Gutenberg (accessed via the Gutendex API)
    Most ebooks are in the public domain in the United States. Some
    items carry specific license terms stated on their individual
    Gutenberg pages. Content remains the property of Project Gutenberg
    and its terms of use apply.

Open Library / Internet Archive (archive.org)
    Catalog metadata is made available under CC BY-SA / ODbL. Public-
    domain scans are free to download; lending items are subject to
    controlled digital lending and require a free account. All rights
    reserved by the Internet Archive where applicable.

Wikisource (zh.wikisource.org, en.wikisource.org)
    Text is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0
    (CC BY-SA 4.0), with attribution requirements stated on each page.
    Individual embedded works may carry their own terms.

Standard Ebooks (standardebooks.org)
    Releases are dedicated to the public domain; source files carry
    their own release notes. The Standard Ebooks name and site design
    remain the property of Standard Ebooks.

CBETA (cbeta.org)
    The Chinese Buddhist canon materials are licensed under Creative
    Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0).
    NON-COMMERCIAL use only; attribution and share-alike required.

wenshuoge (github.com/zhpelo/wenshuoge)
    A public repository of classical Chinese texts. The texts
    themselves are public-domain ancient works; repository structure
    and any editorial additions remain with their author.

daizhigev20 (github.com/garychowcmu/daizhigev20)
    A public-domain corpus of classical Chinese texts. The texts
    themselves are ancient public-domain works; the repository
    compilation remains with its maintainer.

Disclaimer
----------

mu-ebook-scout is a search guide: it indexes public-domain and openly
licensed material, passes through user-configured custom source links
as-is, and downloads files only on explicit user request. Compliance
with each source's license terms, and with the user's local law, is
the responsibility of the user. This project is not affiliated with
any of the sources listed above.
